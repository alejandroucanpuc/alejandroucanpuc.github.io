### Related events

Current organizing and collaboration activities:

* **III OMUM 2026 (Olimpiada Mexicana Universitaria de Matemáticas)**: Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas A.C. (CIMAT), with activities for CIIM delegation selection. Important dates published on the official page include host registration and participant registration in April-May 2026, and selective exams in May and July 2026. Official site: [sites.google.com/cimat.mx/omum-2026/home](https://sites.google.com/cimat.mx/omum-2026/home)
* **59 Congreso Nacional SMM (2026)**: Congreso Nacional de la Sociedad Matemática Mexicana, hosted in the framework of the 50th anniversary of Universidad Autónoma de Tlaxcala (FCBIyT). Official site: [smm.org.mx/congresos](https://smm.org.mx/congresos)
* **Minisymposium at Reunión Anual MexSIAM 2026**: "Nuevas Tendencias en Modelos Matemáticos Aplicados a la Ciencia de Datos" at the Reunión Anual 2026 of the Mexico Section of SIAM, hosted by Universidad Autónoma Benito Juárez de Oaxaca, Facultad de Ciencias. Official site: [mexsiam2026.uabjo.mx/inicio](https://www.mexsiam2026.uabjo.mx/inicio)
