Uncertainty is not a defect in science. It is a record of honesty about what is known, what is estimated, and what still depends on assumptions or limited data.

When communicating science, the useful question is not whether uncertainty exists, but whether it is being described clearly enough for readers to understand the scope of the result.

## Why it matters

* It improves trust.
* It prevents overclaiming.
* It helps people interpret results responsibly.
